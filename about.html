About

This site is run by students. If something is wrong or missing, open an issue or a pull request on GitHub.

We built this guide to make first-year university life a little less overwhelming. The events, answers, and contacts here come from the student body — not from administration — because the people closest to the experience understand it best.

It is open-source and maintained by volunteers. Contributions of any kind — corrections, new events, improved copy — are welcome.
