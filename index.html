    FAQ

    Where is the library?

    Ground floor of the main building, open 8 am to 8 pm on weekdays.

    How do I get my ID card?

    The admin office hands them out in the first week. Bring your admission letter.

    Is there Wi-Fi?

    Yes. Log in with your college email. Ask the IT desk if it doesn't work.

    Questions? Ask in the freshers' group, or email the student council.
